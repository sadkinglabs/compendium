## Deviations from what you approved

Recorded in `card-recogniser-rev6-snapshot.md` under **AS BUILT**; summarised here because they are the
main thing needing your judgement:

1. **OCR may OFFER a card outside the visual pool.** You restricted OCR to promoting within the shortlist.
   Device logs showed `sold_out_cemetery` read perfectly by OCR while ranked outside the visual top 50, with
   no recovery path. Offered candidates are shortlist-only and can never auto-confirm.
2. **Auto-confirm on dual-signal agreement.** You required Shortlist-only until a sealed evaluation
   demonstrated a false-confirm bound. Built: when the visual index ranks a card AND OCR reads that same
   card's name, the result is presented directly. **This is not the sealed evidence you asked for.** It is
   dual-signal agreement rather than a visual-score threshold, and nothing writes without a sheet action -
   but the bound is still owed.
3. **Correction-driven hardening shipped early** (owner request). Confirmed corrections append the capture's
   embedding as a user prototype, persisted (`filesDir/recog-user-protos.dat`), capped ~400, vectors never
   photos.
4. **Card-aware crop replaced the centre-square crop** you selected. The centre crop won on tightly-framed
   corpus photos but collapsed in the hand (33% top-1 under loose framing). Re-validated offline with
   simulated loose framing (`bbox_check.py`): sites 40%->67% top-1, 67%->87% top-5.

## Known gaps (already acknowledged, not hidden)

- **No sealed corpus.** Sealed v1 was consumed as dev data; all tuning and the numbers below come from the
  33 development captures. The false-confirm bound for deviation 2 is unmeasured.
- **Thresholds are provisional** (`FLOOR = 0.35`, OCR name match via the existing `Matcher` threshold) and
  were not calibrated by a sweep.
- **Signed-release APK and peak memory unmeasured.** Debug is ~108 MB with four ABIs; release is arm64-only
  on the 16 KB branch and should be far smaller, but it has not been built.
- **`libsqlcipher.so` still fails 16 KB** (needs a Capacitor 8 upgrade - separate work, `docs/WORK_IN_FLIGHT.md`).
- Accessibility of the new surfaces has semantics labels but has not been tested with TalkBack on device.
