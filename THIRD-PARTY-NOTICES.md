# Third-party notices

Compendium ships third-party work under licences that require their notices to travel with the
software. The authoritative, shipped copy is `src/thirdPartyNotices.js` — it is string **data**
in the production bundle (a source comment would be stripped by minification) and is rendered
in full by the app's Credits screen. This file mirrors it for the source distribution, and
`src/thirdPartyNotices.test.mjs` fails if the two drift apart.
